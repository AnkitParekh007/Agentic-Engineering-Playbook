# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Agentic Engineering Playbook to be an open, practical, and respectful learning community for developers.

## Expected behavior

- be respectful and constructive
- focus feedback on the work, not the person
- help improve clarity, accuracy, and usefulness
- assume good intent while maintaining technical rigor

## Unacceptable behavior

- harassment, abuse, or discrimination
- personal attacks
- bad-faith disruption
- spam or deliberately misleading technical content

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may moderate discussions, reject contributions, or remove participants who violate these standards.

## Scope

This applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and project-associated community spaces.
